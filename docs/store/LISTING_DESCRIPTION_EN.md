Make studying more practical

PlumePilot is a free, open-source assistant for accessing and organising Pegaso, Mercatorum and San Raffaele courses that the user is already authorised to view.

What it offers

• Course progress monitoring and identification of the first incomplete activity
• Configurable controls for playback and course activities
• Local creation of test collections as PDF or interactive HTML, with room for explanations and personal notes
• Generation of course study materials as PDF or EPUB
• Online exam status monitoring
• A floating menu available on home and exam pages, themes and an optional Gaming mode

Why PlumePilot

PlumePilot was born first and foremost from a few everyday needs I kept running into as a student.

I found it tedious to manually click through every video just to keep progressing, download course materials one by one, and, above all, read them on my phone while commuting to work. Then there was the wait after an online exam: days, sometimes weeks, spent repeatedly checking the platform for updates from the examination committee.

Those small frustrations became the foundation for PlumePilot’s main features: autoplay, the ability to combine an entire course’s materials into a single PDF or EPUB, interactive HTML quizzes for practice and personal notes, and commission-status monitoring so that changes are easier to notice.

These are tools I created because I needed them myself first. Sharing them was the natural next step, in the hope that they can make the study journey a little easier for other students too.

Gaming mode adds a lighter touch to studying through EXP, achievements and cosmetic rewards, without changing the core functionality.

Privacy and requirements

PlumePilot does not use analytics, advertising, profiling or telemetry and has no developer-operated server. Processing and generated files remain local.

A valid account on at least one supported platform is required. Automatic actions involving Tests and Objectives are disabled by default and run only after an explicit user choice.

PlumePilot is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or distributed by Multiversity or the supported universities.
